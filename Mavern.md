# Mavern

> **Mavern is an independent creative practice focused on development, art, music, writing, and experimentation. Creativity is the objective.**

## What Mavern is

Mavern is my umbrella organisation: a home for the things I make and publish. It can host blogs, software, games, music, visual art, writing, and experiments across formats.

Mavern is not defined by one product or medium. The work may change; the independent creative practice behind it is the constant.

## Why it exists

To make room for ideas and carry them into finished work. Some projects may be useful, playful, personal, strange, practical, or still exploratory. They do not need to fit one commercial category to belong here.

## How the work should feel

- Independent and self-directed.
- Curious, expressive, and willing to try unfamiliar forms.
- Made with care, with attention to both the idea and its execution.
- Clear and unpretentious when explaining what something is.
- Open about work that is experimental, unfinished, paused, or complete.

## How the site should work

The website is Mavern's home and catalogue. It should help people discover the practice, browse its writing and projects, and find the work itself. Give each project the space and presentation its medium needs while making its relationship to Mavern clear.

Do not make the whole site read like the landing page for one software product. Do not force unrelated work into a single visual template. Use a consistent Mavern identity for navigation, publishing, and attribution; let each project have its own voice and art direction where that serves the work.

## Writing and publishing

Write like the person who made the work: directly, thoughtfully, and in plain language. Explain the idea, the choices behind it, and how someone can experience or use it. Let the work carry its own importance; do not inflate it with promotional claims.

For each release or post, make clear what it is, who made it, when it was published, its current status, and where to find it. Distinguish finished releases from works in progress and experiments.

## Promotion

Promotion exists to help the right people notice, understand, and experience the work. Lead with the work itself: a playable build, a passage, a sound, an image, a useful detail, or the question that started it. Keep the invitation clear and the claims accurate. Do not invent urgency, popularity, results, or a commercial promise that the work does not make.

## Identity in progress

The current site and logo samples are working explorations, not a final identity system. Keep existing artwork intact and treat alternate colors and lockups as options until I choose them. Build recognition through considered, repeated use of the Mavern name and mark rather than adding decoration to every surface.
